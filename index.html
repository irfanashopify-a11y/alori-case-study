1 Project Origin & The Client's Mandate

The real journey began when the founder of ALORI approached me with a crystal-clear, sales-driven mandate:

"I want to build a brand that doesn't just look pretty, but converts visitors into actual sales."

In a saturated women's wellness sector filled with generic "catch-all" hormone pills, ALORI held a breakthrough scientific angle: unbundling cortisol regulation (stress, sleep, burnout) from estrogen balance (cycle regularity, mood, metabolic health). Our mission was to build a digital home that honored luxury beauty standards while relentlessly optimizing for conversions and recurring monthly subscriptions.

2 The 6 Strategic Discovery Questions

To establish alignment and eliminate guesswork before touching development, I conducted an in-depth discovery audit covering six critical pillars:

1. Reference Stores & Visual Benchmark

Question: Do you have any reference stores or inspirations?
Outcome: Client targeted an editorial, warm-neutral apothecary aesthetic reminiscent of cult luxury wellness brands (clean serif typography, organic photography).

2. Product Content & Descriptions

Question: Do you have complete product content, ingredient breakdowns, and photography?
Outcome: Client provided initial product copy, clinical ingredient listings, and studio-grade 3D product renders.

3. Brand Kit & Legal Policies

Question: Do you have your brand kit, fonts, and store legal policies ready?
Outcome: Client delivered complete vector logos, custom font packages, a master 32-slide brand guidelines deck, and legal terms.

4. Technical On-Page SEO

Question: Do you require technical SEO and structured data schema?
Outcome: Implemented comprehensive JSON-LD product schemas, open-graph hierarchy, and semantic headings for Google ranking.

5. Target Launch Timeline

Question: What is your hard launch timeline?
Outcome: Structured an agile multi-week roadmap for design, development, payment integration, and QA testing.

6. Project Budget & App Stack

Question: What is your allocated budget for custom theme builds and app subscriptions?
Outcome: Fully funded budget allocated for custom Shopify Liquid development, recurring billing setup (**Seal Subscriptions**), and performance tuning.

3 Brand Guidelines (32-Slide Master Guide) & Asset Implementation

The client provided a complete brand kit centered around a 32-slide master Brand Guidelines document. Every section of the store strictly adopted this visual language down to the pixel:

Master Delivery: Vector Logos (600x300 px), Fonts, & 32-Slide Brand Guidelines PDF

4 Homepage Architecture: CRO Section-by-Section Breakdown

The homepage was designed as an intentional, high-converting funnel engineered to guide visitors smoothly from awareness to checkout:

1. Hero Header & Immediate Value Proposition

Attention & Hook
  • Clear Value Match: Communicates the core benefit (natural hormone balance) within 3 seconds above the fold.
  • Sticky Incentives: Announcement bar featuring free shipping thresholds and special launch offers.
  • Visual Anchoring: Studio bottle render placed right alongside the primary "Shop Now" call to action.

2. Credibility, Press & Trust Indicators

Skepticism Reduction
  • "As Seen In" Badges: Positions the brand alongside recognized lifestyle authorities and wellness press.
  • Clean Formulation Badges: 100% Plant-Based, Stimulant-Free, Non-GMO, and Lab-Tested certifications build instant credibility.

3. Flagship Products & Bundle Price Anchoring

AOV Maximization
  • Clear Pathway Separation: Distinct separation between Cortisol, Who? and Not Today, Estrogen prevents customer choice fatigue.
  • The Bundle Hook: Highlights The Balanced Baddie Bundle prominently, offering an attractive discount that instantly boosts Average Order Value (AOV).

4. "How It Works" 3-Step Protocol

Routine Clarity & Retention
  • Step 1 (Take it daily): Removes intimidation ("Start your stack every morning—just a few capsules, once a day. No guesswork.").
  • Step 2 (Let it build): Sets realistic expectations on how clinical adaptogens support hormone metabolism over time.
  • Step 3 (Feel the shift): Outlines the 4–8 week window to feel grounded, reinforced by the safety of the 45-Day Guarantee.
  • Brand Identity Match: Built with signature terracotta cards on blush background using sunburst step numbers.

5. Clinical Transparency & Ingredient Education

Objection Handling
  • Deconstructing Adaptogens: Openly reveals clinical dosages of Rhodiola Rosea, Tongkat Ali, Shilajit, Pregnenolone, and BioPerine.
  • Biological Explanation: Explains how cortisol overload triggers facial puffiness, midday crashes, and sleep disruption.

6. "Real Stories. Real Symptoms. Real Relief." (UGC & Social Proof)

Relatable Proof
  • Symptom-Specific Relatability: Shares unfiltered quotes from real women addressing estrogen overload, birth control changes, and corporate stress burnout.
  • Visual Aesthetic: High-contrast terracotta serif typography paired with powder sky-blue backdrop.
  • Product Attribution: Each reviewer's story links directly to the specific formula they used.

7. Founder Story & Human Connection

Brand Affinity
  • Founder Authenticity: Shares Karalyn Zamora’s personal battle with hormonal burnout, establishing an empathetic peer-to-peer relationship.
  • Elevated Editorial: High-end lifestyle imagery and editorial quotes elevate the brand far above sterile generic supplements.

8. Risk Reversals & 45-Day Satisfaction Guarantee

Checkout Confidence
  • 45-Day Money-Back Guarantee: Removes all purchase hesitation for first-time buyers.
  • Self-Serve Subscription Portal: Prominent reassurance that subscriptions can be paused or cancelled anytime.
